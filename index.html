--- layout: single author_profile: true title: "Statistical machine learning for reliable decision-making" permalink: / --- I am a Ph.D. candidate in Statistical Science at Duke University. My research sits at the intersection of reinforcement learning, large language models, and Bayesian inference. I build learning methods that are both practically useful and statistically principled - from stable policy optimization to efficient personalization for LLMs. ## What I work on **Reliable reinforcement learning.** I study optimization stability, robust policy updates, and finite-time guarantees for average-reward and off-policy learning. **LLM post-training and alignment.** My work develops reinforcement-learning methods for reasoning, safety, and data-efficient personalization of large language models. **Bayesian machine learning.** I design efficient inference and uncertainty-aware decision methods for scientific and operational problems. ## Recent highlights - Research intern with Apple Foundation Models, working on reinforcement learning for frontier LLMs (2026). - Developed a reinforcement-learning framework for safe LLM fine-tuning at AWS GenAI; the work appeared at AAAI (2026). - First author of *Implicit Updates for Average-Reward TD Learning* at AISTATS (2026). - Published efficient variational-inference work in the *Journal of Computational and Graphical Statistics* (2025).
