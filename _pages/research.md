My goal is to make learning systems more reliable: stable to optimize, statistically defensible, and useful under real-world feedback constraints.

## Reliable reinforcement learning

I develop reinforcement-learning algorithms that address instability in policy optimization and average-reward settings. This includes finite-time analysis, robust estimators for heavy-tailed policy ratios, and empirical evaluation in robotics and operations-research simulations.

## LLM post-training, safety, and personalization

I work on reinforcement-learning approaches to improve the reasoning, safety, and personalization of large language models. Recent work includes safe LLM fine-tuning with verifiable rewards, stable post-training for frontier models, and a PAC-Bayesian approach to transferring preference information across users.

## Bayesian inference for scientific decisions

My Bayesian ML work focuses on efficient approximate inference and calibrated uncertainty. I have developed variational methods for computer-model calibration and uncertainty-aware active-learning methods for materials science.

## Applications

I enjoy grounding methodological work in consequential applications, including constrained logistics optimization, robotic control, materials discovery, and geospatial environmental monitoring.
